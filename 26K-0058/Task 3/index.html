CAMERA : SONY A6100




DESCRIPTION

The Sony A6100 is a compact mirrorless camera ideal for beginners and enthusiasts. It features a 24.2MP APS-C sensor, fast autofocus with real-time tracking, and 4K video recording. With excellent image quality, user-friendly controls, and wireless connectivity, it’s perfect for capturing stunning photos and smooth videos effortlessly.
